About the branch

A working Post Office counter in Leicester, handling the paperwork, parcels and payments that keep the area ticking over.

Our story

The counter people actually use

Bank branches closed, council offices moved and a lot of everyday admin went online. What did not disappear was the need to hand something to a person: a passport form that has to be right, cash that needs banking, a parcel that has to be tracked.

That is the gap this branch fills. We keep 24 services on one counter so a single visit does the work of three trips, and we keep experienced staff on that counter so the answers are the right ones.

What we are for

Making essential services convenient again

Every visit should be quick, clear and finished — not the first of three attempts.

Get it right first time

Where a service involves an application, we check the details before it is submitted. Catching an error at the counter is far cheaper than catching it six weeks later.

Tell you what to bring

Most wasted journeys come from a missing document. Ring first and we will list exactly what your particular job needs.

Keep the range wide

Passports, post, banking, currency, DVLA and money transfer on one counter, so you are not sent somewhere else.
